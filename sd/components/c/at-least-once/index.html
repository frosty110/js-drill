At-Least-Once Delivery

The default durable-messaging guarantee: a message is redelivered until acknowledged, so it may arrive more than once but never zero times.

Async & Messaging signature mechanism 4 problems

Reach for it when

  • Losing a message is unacceptable and duplicates are survivable
  • Consumers can be made idempotent
  • The prompt involves deliveries, notifications or webhooks that must not be dropped

Don't reach for it when

  • Side effects are irreversible and cannot be deduplicated — charging a card twice is not a rounding error
  • Duplicate suppression cannot be implemented anywhere in the pipeline

What it costs you

  • Every consumer must carry deduplication or idempotency logic
  • Retry policy is a real design surface: interval, ceiling, jitter, give-up
  • Duplicate rate is invisible unless you measure it

How it breaks

  • Consumer acknowledges before the work is durable and the message is lost anyway
  • Retry storm after a downstream outage, amplifying load exactly when it is weakest
  • Poison message consuming the entire retry budget forever with no dead-letter path

Instead, consider

  • Exactly-Once (Effectively-Once) — Effectively-once via idempotency keys and transactional sinks — more machinery, stronger promise.
  • Dead-Letter Queue — The required companion: somewhere for a message that has failed enough times to go.

Used in 4 design problems

  • Design a Notification System — A notification may arrive twice but must never be silently dropped, which is what sets the deduplication requirement downstream.
  • Design a Distributed Job Scheduler — A scheduled job may fire twice if a worker dies mid-run, so job bodies have to tolerate re-execution by construction.
  • Design a Webhook Delivery System — The contract offered to receivers — retried until acknowledged — which is why every delivery carries an id they can dedupe on.
  • Design a Financial Crime Alert Investigation System — Alert delivery is retried by the bank and by the transport, so the pipeline assumes duplicates by default and makes the investigation step safe to repeat rather than trying to deduplicate at the edge.
