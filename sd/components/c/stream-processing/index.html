Stream Processing

Continuous computation over an unbounded event stream — windowed aggregation, enrichment and joins applied as data arrives.

Reach for it when

  • The prompt needs near-real-time aggregates over a firehose
  • Storing every raw event to query later is too expensive or too slow
  • Results must update continuously rather than on a schedule

Don't reach for it when

  • A nightly batch would satisfy the requirement — batch is dramatically simpler to operate and reason about
  • The computation needs arbitrary access to all historical data at once

What it costs you

  • Windowing, watermarks and late-data policy are decisions you cannot avoid making
  • Stateful operators need checkpointed state, which is the hard operational part
  • Reprocessing history means replaying the stream, and that has to be designed for

How it breaks

  • Late events arriving after their window closed and being silently dropped
  • Unbounded state growth in a keyed operator with no TTL
  • Checkpoint failures forcing a restart that re-emits already-published results

Instead, consider

  • Pre-Aggregation & Rollups — If you only need counters, a pre-aggregated store may be enough without a stream framework.
  • Time-Series Store — Store the points and let the query engine aggregate, when freshness requirements are looser.

Used in 4 design problems

  • Design an Ad Click Aggregator — Windowed counting over the click firehose, where watermarks and late-event policy decide what a given 'minute' actually contains.
  • Design an Observability Platform — Telemetry is enriched, sampled and aggregated in flight, because storing everything raw and querying later meets neither the cost nor latency bar.
  • Design a Feature Store — Real-time features are derived from event streams as they arrive — what makes an online feature fresher than a nightly batch job.
  • Design a Content Moderation Pipeline — Uploads flow through a scoring pipeline continuously, so moderation decisions land within seconds of publish rather than on a schedule.
