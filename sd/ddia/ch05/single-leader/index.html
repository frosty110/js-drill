Single-leader replication, lag, and failover

Ordered log flow study sheet

What this sheet shows

The leader assigns a single order and streams a replication log to followers. Synchronous followers improve durability but can block writes; asynchronous followers reduce latency but can lose acknowledged writes and serve stale reads after a leader failure.

Trace one write through the leader and log, then connect acknowledgement timing to lag, stale reads, and data loss.

Trace the flow

  1. Write the leader — The client sends all mutations to the elected leader.
  2. Append and apply — The leader records the write and applies it in log order.
  3. Replicate — Followers consume the log synchronously or asynchronously and expose a replay position.
  4. Acknowledge — The durability contract depends on which follower acknowledgements are required before success.
  5. Serve reads safely — Use leader pinning or log-position checks for read-your-writes and monotonic reads.
  6. Fail over — Detect failure, elect the most caught-up follower, fence the old leader, and reconfigure clients.
