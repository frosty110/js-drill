Fanout Write Flow

Write flow study sheet

What this sheet shows

A Twitter/Facebook-style home feed that shows each user a merged, ranked stream of posts from the accounts they follow. The signature challenge is the fan-out tradeoff: precompute feeds on write (push) vs. assemble them on read (pull), and the hybrid needed to survive high-follower 'celebrity'.

Trace the flow

  1. New Post — New Post; then continue to Celebrity Threshold.
  2. Celebrity Threshold — Celebrity Threshold; then continue to Fan-out Queue.
  3. Fan-out Queue — Fan-out Queue; then continue to Follower Feed Cache.
  4. Follower Feed Cache — Follower Feed Cache; then continue to Posts Store.
  5. Posts Store — Posts Store; then continue to Feed Read.
  6. Feed Read — Feed Read; then continue to Merge.
