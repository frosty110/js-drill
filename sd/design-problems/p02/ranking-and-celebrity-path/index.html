Ranking And Celebrity Path

Focused mechanism study sheet

What this sheet shows

Write path: a normal user's post is pushed into follower feeds via the queue; a celebrity's post is just stored once, no fan-out.

Trace the flow

  1. Celebrity Post — Celebrity Post; then continue to Posts Store.
  2. Posts Store — Posts Store; then continue to Replicated Hot Cache.
  3. Replicated Hot Cache — Replicated Hot Cache; then continue to Reader Fleet.
  4. Reader Fleet — Reader Fleet; then continue to Push Bypassed.
  5. Push Bypassed — Push Bypassed; verify the result and expose the outcome.

What this belongs to

A Twitter/Facebook-style home feed that shows each user a merged, ranked stream of posts from the accounts they follow. The signature challenge is the fan-out tradeoff: precompute feeds on write (push) vs. assemble them on read (pull), and the hybrid needed to survive high-follower 'celebrity' accounts.
