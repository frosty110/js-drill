Channel Delivery Flow

Focused mechanism study sheet

What this belongs to

A fan-out service that delivers notifications across many channels (mobile push via APNS/FCM, email, SMS, in-app) reliably and at spike scale. The signature challenge is a queue-decoupled, per-channel worker pipeline with user preferences, dedup/idempotency, retries with backoff + dead-letter, and priority lanes.
