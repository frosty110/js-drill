Notification Ingest Flow

Focused mechanism study sheet

What this sheet shows

Fan-out multiplies work: one 'notify user' event may hit 2-3 channels, so worker-side dispatch QPS is a small multiple of ingest QPS.

Trace the flow

  1. Notification Intent — Notification Intent; then continue to Fan-out Worker.
  2. Fan-out Worker — Fan-out Worker; then continue to Push Queue.
  3. Push Queue — Push Queue; then continue to Email Queue.
  4. Email Queue — Email Queue; then continue to SMS Queue.
  5. SMS Queue — SMS Queue; then continue to Push Adapter.
  6. Push Adapter — Push Adapter; then continue to Email Adapter.

What this belongs to

A fan-out service that delivers notifications across many channels (mobile push via APNS/FCM, email, SMS, in-app) reliably and at spike scale. The signature challenge is a queue-decoupled, per-channel worker pipeline with user preferences, dedup/idempotency, retries with backoff + dead-letter, and priority lanes.
