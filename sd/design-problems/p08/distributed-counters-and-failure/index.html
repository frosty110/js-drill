What this sheet shows

A shared service (or middleware) that caps how many requests a client may make per time window across a fleet of servers. The signature challenge is the DISTRIBUTED part: a counter shared across nodes, the read-modify-write race, and fixing it atomically — plus choosing an algorithm (token bucket.
