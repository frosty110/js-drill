Contention Expiry And Recovery

Failure and recovery study sheet

What this sheet shows

Release expired holds reliably (TTL sweeper / expiry job) so unsold seats don't leak.

Trace the flow

  1. Active Hold — Active Hold; then continue to Payment Orchestrator.
  2. Payment Orchestrator — Payment Orchestrator; then continue to Payment Gateway.
  3. Payment Gateway — Payment Gateway; then continue to Mark Sold.
  4. Mark Sold — Mark Sold; then continue to Release Hold.
  5. Release Hold — Release Hold; then continue to Expiry Queue.
  6. Expiry Queue — Expiry Queue; then continue to Expiry Worker.

What this belongs to

Sell reserved seats for events under extreme flash-sale concurrency. The signature challenge is preventing two buyers from booking the same seat — via a temporary hold with a TTL backed by a concurrency-control choice (pessimistic lock vs optimistic vs reservation) — while a virtual waiting room absorbs the spike and payment completes within the hold window.
