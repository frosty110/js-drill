Refill Approval Flow

Write flow study sheet

What this sheet shows

Subscriptions must re-validate every cycle: the prescription can expire or exhaust its refills, and a new medication started since signup can turn a safe refill into an interaction. The subscription stores intent, never an approval.

Trace the flow

  1. Subscription Intent — Subscription Intent; then continue to Refill Scheduler.
  2. Refill Scheduler — Refill Scheduler; then continue to Rx Active and Refills Left?.
  3. Rx Active and Refills Left? — Rx Active and Refills Left?; then continue to Notify Patient and Prescriber.
  4. Notify Patient and Prescriber — Notify Patient and Prescriber; then continue to Current Safety Check.
  5. Current Safety Check — Current Safety Check; then continue to Current Medication List.
  6. Current Medication List — Current Medication List; then continue to Create Refill Order.

What this belongs to

An online pharmacy marketplace with recurring refills, where the signature challenge is not scale but SAFETY correctness: no patient may ever be dispensed a medication that is contraindicated for them. A fail-closed clinical validation gate sits synchronously in the order-placement write path, reading a strongly-consistent view of the patient's active medication list, and re-runs on every subscription cycle because a refill that was safe at signup can become unsafe months later.
