Two-stage aggregation for a whale advertiser

Mechanism study sheet

What this sheet shows

One advertiser at 40% of traffic saturates a single partition. Salting the key into sub-keys spreads it across partitions at the cost of a merge stage.

Make it obvious that adding partitions cannot split a single key, and show the shape that can.

Trace the flow

  1. Detect the skew — Per-partition throughput — not the healthy aggregate — reveals the hot key.
  2. Salt the key — ad_42 becomes ad_42:0..N, so one logical key hashes to many partitions.
  3. Aggregate in parallel — Each salted bucket keeps its own local counter on its own consumer.
  4. Merge in stage two — A second aggregation sums the salted buckets back into one (ad_id, minute) value.

What this belongs to

Ingest a firehose of ad-click events and serve near-real-time aggregated counts that are accurate enough to bill on. The signature challenge is counting correctly under at-least-once delivery, out-of-order events, and advertiser hot keys.
