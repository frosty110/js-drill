Three repair mechanisms on three timescales

Failure study sheet

What this sheet shows

Read repair fixes what is read, hinted handoff covers brief outages, and Merkle-tree anti-entropy is the only mechanism that ever repairs data nobody touches.

Show that no single repair mechanism is sufficient, and which one covers cold data.

Trace the flow

  1. Repair on read — A quorum read compares versions and asynchronously pushes the newest to stale replicas.
  2. Hold a hint — If a replica is briefly down, a peer accepts the write and replays it on recovery.
  3. Compare Merkle roots — Replicas exchange a hash tree over a key range; equal roots mean the range is in sync.
  4. Descend only where they differ — Unequal subtrees are walked down to isolate the differing keys.
  5. Sync the differing keys — Only those keys move, not the whole range.

What this belongs to

Build a Dynamo/Cassandra-style durable key-value store: consistent hashing for placement, quorum replication for tunable consistency, and anti-entropy to repair the divergence that leaderless writes inevitably create.
