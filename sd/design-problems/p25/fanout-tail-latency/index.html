Why p99 is the slowest shard

Failure study sheet

What this sheet shows

The coordinator waits for every shard, so one garbage-collection pause anywhere sets the latency for the whole query.

Show how fan-out multiplies tail risk rather than averaging it.

Trace the flow

  1. Fan out to all shards — Every query touches every shard by design.
  2. Most respond quickly — Typical shards answer in tens of milliseconds.
  3. One stalls — A GC pause or hot cache miss makes one shard slow.
  4. The coordinator waits — Total latency is the maximum, not the mean.
  5. Hedge the request — Re-send to a replica after a short delay and take the first response.

What this belongs to

Serve full-text queries over billions of documents in under a second. The signature challenge is that the inverted index inverts the obvious data layout — you store term to documents rather than document to terms — and everything else follows from making that index shardable, rankable and refreshable.
