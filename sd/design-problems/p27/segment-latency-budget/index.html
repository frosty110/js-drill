Where 20-30 seconds of HLS latency comes from

Mechanism study sheet

What this sheet shows

A segment cannot be served until it is complete, and the player buffers several before playing — so the floor is roughly three segment durations.

Show that the delay is structural rather than slow infrastructure.

Trace the flow

  1. Capture the moment — The camera produces frames continuously.
  2. Wait for a complete segment — An HTTP object is unservable until finished: one full segment duration.
  3. Publish and propagate — Packaging and CDN distribution add a little more.
  4. Buffer to survive jitter — Players hold about three segments before starting playback.
  5. Attack the right constraint — Low-latency HLS delivers partial segments rather than shrinking them.

What this belongs to

Broadcast one creator's video to millions of concurrent viewers with only seconds of delay, plus a live chat that fans out to the same audience. The signature challenge is that latency and scale pull in opposite directions — the CDN that makes a million viewers affordable is the same thing that adds the delay interactivity can't tolerate.
