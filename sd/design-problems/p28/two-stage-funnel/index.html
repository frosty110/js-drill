Why retrieval and ranking are separate

Mechanism study sheet

What this sheet shows

The accurate ranker costs about a millisecond per candidate, which fits five hundred candidates and never fits a billion.

Show the same funnel that appears in search and RAG, and why.

Trace the flow

  1. Start from the catalogue — Billions of eligible videos, no graph to narrow them.
  2. Retrieve cheaply — ANN over embeddings, precomputed trending lists, follows.
  3. Reserve for exploration — A few percent for under-served content.
  4. Rank expensively — A deep model scores a few hundred survivors.
  5. Return the top few — Only a handful ever reach the user.

What this belongs to

Serve an endless, personalized stream of short videos where the recommendation engine — not a social graph — decides what plays next. The signature challenge is that the feed must be ranked per user in tens of milliseconds while the video for the *next* swipe is already downloading.
