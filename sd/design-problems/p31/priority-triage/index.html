Triage when the queue exceeds capacity

Failure study sheet

What this sheet shows

FIFO treats a viral threat identically to a three-view meme. Ranking by severity times predicted reach puts scarce human attention where damage accrues fastest.

Show why arrival order is the worst possible allocation under overload.

Trace the flow

  1. Measure the overflow — Arrival exceeds review capacity, so something must be dropped or deferred.
  2. Score expected harm — Severity multiplied by predicted reach from follower count and early velocity.
  3. Review the top slice — Human attention goes to the highest-damage items first.
  4. Apply an explicit tail policy — Auto-action conservatively or expire with an audit record.
  5. Alert on queue age — Age percentiles reveal starvation that queue length hides.

What this belongs to

Screen user-generated content at platform scale using classifiers plus a human review queue. The signature challenge is that both error directions cause real harm, so the design is about routing uncertainty to humans rather than pushing a classifier toward perfect accuracy it will never reach.
