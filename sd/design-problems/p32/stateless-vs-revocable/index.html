The tension the two-token split resolves

Comparison study sheet

What this sheet shows

Statelessness is what makes verification free and revocation impossible, so the two properties belong on two different credentials.

Make the central tradeoff explicit before presenting the solution.

Trace the flow

  1. Want free verification — A self-contained signed token needs no lookup.
  2. Discover the cost — There is nothing to change, so it cannot be revoked.
  3. Want instant revocation — A lookup per request gives complete control.
  4. Discover that cost too — It reintroduces the round trip the token avoided.
  5. Split the credential — Short stateless access token, long stateful refresh token.

What this belongs to

Authenticate users once and let every service verify them without a round trip, while still being able to revoke access immediately. The signature challenge is that stateless tokens and instant revocation are in direct tension — the property that makes JWTs scale is exactly the property that makes them impossible to cancel.
